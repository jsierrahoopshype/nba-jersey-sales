Methodology

  • U.S. lists are every list except Europe only, Philippines only and the 2013 worldwide release (U.S. top 10). All U.S. counts, peaks, streaks, No. 1s and points use U.S. lists only.
  • Jersey points: 11 minus rank for the top 10 of each U.S. list (No. 1 = 10, No. 10 = 1). Ranks 11 and lower score 0, so lists of different lengths compare fairly.
  • Full U.S. list lengths in the source: 15, 20 and 25 spots.
  • Known gaps, shown as they are: End of regular season, 2001 (No. 1 only); End of regular season, 2002 (No. 1 only); Midseason, Jan 2005 (empty); Postseason, Jun 2006 (No. 1 only).
  • A list with only the No. 1, or no data, does not break or extend a player's run and is skipped when comparing moves.
  • Players listed with more than one team (traded during the sales period) count for each team listed.
  • Rows marked with a small note carry the source flag (for example, from a 2025 HoopsHype article only, or a team filled in).
  • Production rank: league rank in points + rebounds + assists per game that regular season, min. 20 games (nba-player-data). Salary, awards, MVP votes, birthplace and road attendance come from other HoopsMatic datasets; a player or season that did not match cleanly is left blank.
