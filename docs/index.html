    Methodology

    • U.S. lists are every list except Europe only and Philippines only. All U.S. counts, rankings, moves and points use U.S. lists only.
    • The Oct 3, 2013 list was checked against the official NBA release: its ten players are the release's U.S. top 10 for the full 2012-13 season (the worldwide, China, Latin America, Europe and Philippines lists in the same release are not in the source). It now counts as a U.S. list of the 2012-13 season.
    • Season ranking (the main measure): for each season, every complete U.S. list of that season is averaged. A player missing from a list counts one spot below its last place (missing from a 15-player list = 16). The ranking goes as deep as the season's shortest complete list. Ties: better single best rank, then better rank on the later list; still tied = shared position. Lists with only a No. 1 (2001, 2002) give that season's No. 1 only; the Jun 2006 No. 1 only list is not counted because 2005-06 has two complete lists. The empty Jan 2005 list is skipped. International lists never count.
    • No. 1, Top 5 and Top 10 seasons count season rankings. No. 1, Top 5 and Top 10 lists count every U.S. list.
    • Jersey ranking points: 11 minus rank for the top 10 of each season ranking (No. 1 = 10, No. 10 = 1). Every-list points apply the same scoring to each U.S. list. Team points go to the team on the player's last list of the season.
    • Change compares two adjacent complete U.S. lists. A player on both gets the exact move, whatever the two lengths (No. 12 on a 15-player list to No. 8 on a 10-player list = up 4). A player absent from the next list dropped out only if the previous rank fits inside that list; No. 12, then absent from a 10-player list, is not compared. First appearance is a first U.S. list; Re-entry is a return after missing the previous complete list. International lists get no change.
    • Complete U.S. list lengths in the source: 10, 15, 20 and 25 spots. Known gaps, shown as they are: End of regular season, 2001 (incomplete, No. 1 only); End of regular season, 2002 (incomplete, No. 1 only); Midseason, Jan 2005 (empty); Postseason, Jun 2006 (incomplete, No. 1 only). A team's stretch without a ranked player counts complete lists only.
    • Players listed with more than one team (traded during the sales period) count for each team listed. Rows with a small note carry the source flag.
    • Popularity vs. awards: each season, every player with an All-NBA team or MVP votes gets a league-wide award rank: All-NBA team, then MVP voting place (1st team ahead of 2nd, 2nd ahead of 3rd, any team ahead of none). Players with neither show No award votes. Data from nba-player-data (awards.json and awardVotes.json); it covers 2000-01 to 2025-26, every season with a jersey list. No All-Star data.
    • Salary, birthplace and road attendance come from other HoopsMatic datasets; a player or season that did not match cleanly is left blank.
